# Message outlines

**Outlines**, not texts to copy: the AI fills them in with the job ad, the application file and `_CONTEXTE.md`, then adapts them to the user's voice. A generic message shows; a message that picks up the ad's own words and answers them with evidence gets read.

## Common rules

- **Channel limit**: the one shown on the form is what counts (counter under the field). Measure with `{{CMD}} count <file>` (or the `count` tool) and note the length in the application file. Rough guide: an Indeed form (including Glassdoor "Easy Apply") takes up to 4,000 characters; a LinkedIn connection note is very short (a few hundred characters at most); a first message on a freelance platform is best kept to one phone screen.
- **Structure**: an opening that answers the ad (not "I am writing to apply for…"); two or three pieces of evidence from `_CONTEXTE.md`, with figures where possible; any obvious gap owned up to in one sentence; the conditions (availability or notice period, remote work, pay if the channel lends itself to it); a close that suggests a next step (a call, a slot).
- **Honesty**: nothing that cannot be claimed under `_CONTEXTE.md`. No superlatives without evidence.
- **Format**: plain text, no Markdown (forms do not render it). Give it to the user in the "text to copy and paste" format of `AGENTS.md`; keep the version actually sent in the application file.
- **Tone**: polite and direct, first names if the ad or the recruiter uses them; "Dear Hiring Manager" only when no name can be found.

## 1. Application through a form (permanent role or contract, message alongside the CV)

```
Hello {first name, or "Dear Hiring Manager"},

{Opening: what the ad is looking for, in its own words, and in one sentence why this is my home ground.}

{Evidence 1: an achievement that answers the main requirement, with a figure.}
{Evidence 2: an achievement that answers another requirement or the context (sector, size, way of working).}
{Possible gap, owned up to: "I haven't worked with X yet; I'd approach it the way I picked up Y in Z."}

{Conditions: availability or notice period, remote work or location, contract.}

{Close: "I'd be glad to talk it through in a first call." + First name LAST NAME, phone.}
```

## 2. Reply to a freelance brief (Upwork, Malt, a platform or e-mail)

```
Hello {first name if known},

{A short restatement of the need, to show it is understood, and a question or a point of attention that proves experience.}

{One or two comparable contracts: context, what I delivered, the outcome.}

{Proposal: availability, rhythm (days per week), remote or on site, day rate or "to be agreed after a call" depending on the channel.}

{Close: suggest a 20-minute call this week.}
```

## 3. Message to a recruiter or an agency (LinkedIn, e-mail)

```
Hello {first name},

{Why them: the ad or role you saw, or the sector they recruit for.}
{Who I am in one sentence: occupation, years, speciality, main piece of evidence.}
{What I am looking for: type of role, contract, location or remote, availability.}

{Close: CV attached or link, suggestion of a call.}
```

## 4. Follow-up after applying (no reply, delay from the "Conditions" section, otherwise 7 days)

```
Hello {first name, or "Dear Hiring Manager"},

I'm following up on my application for the {job title} role, sent on {date}.
{Something new or a useful detail, in one sentence, if there is one.}
I'm still very interested in the role and happy to talk {availability}.

{First name LAST NAME}
```

## 5. Thank-you after an interview (the same day or the next)

```
Hello {first name},

Thank you for our conversation on {day} about the {job title} role.
{A specific point from the discussion and what it confirms about my interest.}
{If a question was left open: the answer, in two sentences.}

I look forward to hearing about the next steps.
{First name LAST NAME}
```

## 6. Cover letter (only if the form requires one)

One page, same material as message 1, in three paragraphs: what the ad is looking for and why this is my home ground; two achievements that prove it; what I bring to this particular team and the next step I suggest. Header with contact details, date, and the recipient if known.
